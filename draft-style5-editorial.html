02 — AI Filmmaking

Built with GenAI

Where generative tools meet a director's eye for pacing and tone.

Entire worlds, generated and directed — from a single prompt to a finished cut.

I build cinematic shorts completely from scratch using AI image and video tools, then shape them in the edit: sequencing generated shots, designing motion, grading color and scoring sound until they feel like real films.

My focus is experimental GenAI workflows — rapid concepting, image-to-video, and stitching model outputs into a coherent visual language with intentional rhythm and story.
